Who We Are

Central Primary School Misau was established in 1919 and has played an important role in educating generations of pupils and contributing to the development of Misau and its surrounding communities.

The Central Primary School Misau Old Boys Association is an umbrella association established to bring together former pupils across different graduating sets.

The Association promotes friendship, unity, mutual support, educational development, community service and the preservation of the school's rich heritage.

The Class of 1988 is one of the graduating sets whose members initiated the 2026 association meeting and contributed to the idea of establishing this digital platform to strengthen communication among former pupils.

Our Mission

To promote friendship, unity, mutual support and meaningful collaboration among members of the CPS Misau Class of 1988.

Our Vision

To build a united and enduring association that inspires excellence, strengthens relationships and creates lasting value for members and future generations.

Our Core Values

Friendship, unity, integrity, respect, cooperation, responsibility and excellence guide our association.

Our Objectives

The principles that guide our association and collective activities.

Promote Unity
